Brand Guidelines · v1.0

Paraya.

Shelter for the outliers.

Paraya is a media production studio built on one conviction: the outsider's view is the truest one. These guidelines set down how that conviction should look, sound, and behave — in a deck, on a set, on a hoodie, or on the tech portal to come.

01

Origin & meaning

The name is the brand strategy. Everything else in this document is downstream of it.

Paraya means outsider: someone who stands apart, who sees differently. The word carries roots that run deeper than a tagline — echoes of the Banjara, India's nomadic drumming communities, and of the Hindi sense of being "outside the family." It is not a wound. It is a vantage point.

"Being Paraya is not about being excluded. It is about choosing authenticity over conformity."
Paraya
The outsider

Stands apart. Sees differently. Refuses to blend in. The starting condition — the raw material every client, creator and crew member brings into the studio.

Paya (पाया)
The one who found

To find. To discover. To arrive. Paraya's work is the mechanism that moves someone from the left column to the right — from unseen to understood.

The wordmark carries the same idea one layer deeper: set as "PaRTaya," it hides the word ART inside Paraya, capitalized, unmissable once you see it.

PaRTaya

On the live site, clicking the "Paraya" portion of the header also routes to a separate Paya destination (the tech/talent marketplace) with an accompanying color shift that signals the change of context. Treat both as permanent pieces of brand behavior, not one-off details.

03

Voice & tone

Introspective and poetic, but never precious. Paraya writes like it's talking to one person, not a market segment.

IntrospectiveDirectCinematic UnhurriedWarm, not sentimentalConfident without volume

Do

  • Write short declarative sentences, then let one longer one breathe.
  • Use "we" and speak to "you" — the client is a partner, addressed directly.
  • Let a sentence fragment land on its own. Like this.
  • Ground abstract values in a concrete action: "no copy-paste formulas."

Don't

  • Reach for agency jargon: "synergy," "content ecosystem," "value-add."
  • Oversell with superlatives stacked on superlatives.
  • Flatten "outsider" into generic quirky-brand irreverence.
  • Let poetic language replace a concrete claim about the work.

06

Typography

EB Garamond Extra Bold is the locked brand typeface, straight from Ritu's spec sheet. The wordmark itself is hand-lettered, not set type — EB Garamond is what carries the voice everywhere the logo can't reach.

EB Garamond, Extra BoldLocked

Shelter for the outliers

Display headlines, pull quotes, the tagline treatment. Use at large display sizes — Extra Bold only, it's a display cut, not a text weight.

EB Garamond, Regular / ItalicWorking extension

Every story deserves a place to belong.

Long-form body copy, captions, the tagline arc's italic. Not in the locked sheet — Ritu specified Extra Bold only. Recommended so the family reads at body sizes; confirm before adopting.

The live parayaproductions.com build currently runs on Manrope, a geometric sans, for all UI text. That's a placeholder from the temporary site, not the brand spec — flag it for replacement when the Paraya.com rebuild starts. Where a UI needs a workhorse sans EB Garamond can't provide (buttons, form fields, dense data), pair it with a plain, quiet sans rather than reaching for Manrope by default. Confirm the choice with Ritu rather than carrying the placeholder forward by habit.
07

Logo & mark

Hand-lettered, not set in type — that's the point. A studio built on refusing templates doesn't logo itself in a template font.

The wordplay
PaRTaya

Set as "PaRTaya," the capitalized letters spell ART inside the outsider's name. The logotype does the same work as the etymology in Section 01, one layer down.

The ornament

The ring-and-tassel mark hanging off the wordmark and the monogram nods to Banjara jewelry, the nomadic community the name partly draws from. It is a fixed part of the mark, not a seasonal decoration.

Lockup choice

Use the primary lockup (tagline + ornament) wherever there's room to breathe — title cards, decks, print. Drop to the secondary, tagline-free lockup for favicons, merch tags, and anywhere under ~120px wide.

रा

On Outsider Slate

रा

On Found Terracotta

The monogram, रा (Ra), the first syllable of Paraya in Devanagari, stands alone for anywhere the full wordmark won't fit: app icons, social avatars, a stitched merch tag.

Minimum size: keep the monogram no smaller than ~32px on screen or 10mm in print. Below that, the tassel detail disappears and it reads as a blob. Clear space around any lockup: no less than the height of the wordmark's "T" crossbar on all sides.

Never

  • ✕Re-color the wordmark outside slate / terracotta / white / black.
  • ✕Stretch it off its native ratio.
  • ✕Redraw the ART emphasis in a different weight.
  • ✕Place a color lockup over imagery without a scrim.
08

Imagery

Documentary first. Every frame should look caught, not staged, even when it's lit like a film.

Light

Low-key, cinematic

Real shadow. Avoid flat, evenly-lit "content" lighting — Paraya frames like a film still, not a feed grid.

Subject

Real people, mid-thought

Real people over models where possible. Faces mid-thought, not mid-pose.

Emotion

Raw over polished

A shot earns its place by carrying feeling. Reference: The Outsiders, the studio's flagship documentary series.
