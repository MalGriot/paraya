Website Development · Told in Chapters

Paraya Website Development

Six focused monthly releases, each a real and visible piece of the Paraya website, with no full-project commitment required upfront.

Prepared forRitu Singhal, Paraya
Prepared byMal Griot
Investment₹25,000 / chapter
Total scope₹1,50,000 (6 chapters)
A

How this works

Instead of committing to the entire website at once, development happens through six focused monthly releases. Each chapter is ₹25,000, and each one produces a meaningful, visible part of the overall Paraya website, allowing priorities to evolve as the business develops.

Payment is made at the beginning of each chapter. There's no requirement to commit to all six at once; the next chapter is scheduled whenever the current one is complete and Paraya is ready to continue.

B

The six chapters

1

Presence

₹25,000

What we'll build

A live, on-brand Paraya.com (wordmark, tagline, positioning, and a direct route to book a consultation), plus the technical foundation (hosting, CMS, analytics) the rest of the site builds on.

What will be available at the end

Paraya.com is live and bookable, immediately.

2

Core Experience

₹25,000

What we'll build

The full homepage, including an interactive hero built around Paraya's "shelter for the outliers" identity, and the signature Paya gateway: the color-shift transition that signals the move into Paya's world.

What will be available at the end

A homepage that carries the brand's full personality, and a working, permanent Paya gateway.

3

Content & Verticals

₹25,000

What we'll build

The About/Philosophy page, the Work/Portfolio page, and the Contact/Consultation booking page, with all six of Paraya's business verticals made visible and legible across the site.

What will be available at the end

A complete core site. The page structures, layouts, and interactions will be built in this chapter, with final copy, photography, and project details incorporated as they're supplied.

4

Merch Experience

₹25,000

What we'll build

The full merch section: product pages for all seven pieces, the catalogue presentation, and a Linktree-style navigation layer connecting Instagram to the site.

What will be available at the end

A complete, browsable merch experience, ready to go live in full once the catalogue shoot and final product details are in.

5

Commerce & Paya Connection

₹25,000

What we'll build

Direct checkout and payment on Paraya.com, and the connection of the Paya gateway to Paya's live destination once that team's platform is ready.

What will be available at the end

A working store, and a fully connected Paya gateway.

6

Integration & Launch

₹25,000

What we'll build

Full testing across every page and device, performance checks, and hands-on launch support.

What will be available at the end

A fully verified, launch-ready Paraya website.

E

Content & asset preparation

The website build progresses alongside the supply of copy, project descriptions, photography, video, team information, merch information, and brand references. This is normal project workflow: development and content-gathering happen in parallel, not one after the other.

Each chapter is built using placeholders where appropriate, so the structure, design, and functionality can move forward without waiting on final materials. Final publication of asset-dependent sections (like the Work/Portfolio page or the merch catalogue) happens as the relevant approved content comes in.

A few pieces also depend on decisions or setup from outside providers: Calendly for booking, a payment processor for merch, hosting/CMS/analytics providers, and Paya's own team for their side of the gateway connection. These are handled as each chapter reaches them.
