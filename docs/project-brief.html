01

What this is

Paraya asked for a website rebuild, but the brief underneath it is bigger: unify six business lines, launch merch, and stand up a hidden gateway to a second product. This document sets the scope, sequence, and open questions before design and build begin.

Everything here is drawn from the 28 August kickoff call and the discovery form Ritu completed the same day. Where the two sources disagree or leave something open, it's flagged in Section 12 rather than guessed at.

02

Goals & primary action

Audience

Brands & clients

The site is written and structured for people booking Paraya for work, not a general portfolio audience.

Primary action

Book a consultation

Every path on the site should have a short route back to the consultation booking flow.

Beyond that single conversion goal, the site needs to do three things at once: showcase the work and philosophy, sell merch, and hold the door open to Paya, without any one of the three crowding out the others.

09

The Paya gateway

Confirmed as permanent brand behavior, not a one-off Easter egg: clicking the "Paraya" portion of the wordmark routes to a separate Paya destination, with a color shift signaling the change of context: slate/terracotta outward, shifting on arrival at Paya.

Scope boundary: Paya's portal is designed and built by a separate team. It is not part of this site's build, in Phase One or Phase Two. This site's only responsibility is the gateway itself: the click-through and the color shift. Phase One points that gateway at a placeholder; Phase Two just repoints it at wherever the live Paya site ends up, once that team ships it (targeted ~60 days from kickoff).

11

Voice, in three lines

Introspective and poetic, but never precious. Paraya writes like it's talking to one person, not a market segment.

Do

Short declarative sentences, then one longer one that breathes. "We" and "you": the client is a partner. Ground abstract values in a concrete action.

Don't

Agency jargon: "synergy," "content ecosystem." Superlatives stacked on superlatives. Poetic language standing in for a concrete claim about the work.

Decisions already made

→

Domain: the new build ships on Paraya.com, dropping the Paraya Productions domain entirely.

→

Merch channel: sold direct on Paraya.com, not through an external storefront.

→

Paya gateway: the color-shift click-through is permanent brand behavior, not a launch-only flourish.
